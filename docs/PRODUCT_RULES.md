# Product and trust rules

## Audience and initial scope
- Market: India.
- Initial audience hypothesis: women aged 18–34.
- Initial price focus: products around ₹800 or less; this is a discovery filter, not a promise that every product will always be below it.
- Start broad across everyday western and ethnic wear; narrow categories only after audience and performance evidence.

## Evidence fields for each product
- Product title and retailer.
- Canonical product URL.
- Displayed price and currency.
- Date/time checked.
- Fabric/material details as stated by retailer.
- Size range and sizing guidance where available.
- Return/exchange policy source.
- Review summary only when based on accessible, attributable review evidence.
- Availability caveat where relevant.
- Affiliate status and required disclosure.
- Unknown/unverified fields explicitly marked.

## Review states
1. Added — URL received, not checked.
2. Researching — collecting permitted evidence.
3. Needs human review — evidence captured, claims not approved.
4. Approved — checked and ready for a human-authored content draft.
5. Rejected/stale — unsuitable, unavailable or evidence too weak.

## Editorial rules
- No invented ratings, reviews, fabric quality, durability, bestseller labels, discounts or trend claims.
- Distinguish retailer claims from independent evidence.
- A low price alone does not prove value or quality.
- Show uncertainty when evidence is incomplete.
- Recheck volatile facts such as price and availability before publishing.
- Do not publish automatically in the initial release.

## Early scoring hypothesis (not validated)
Potential internal prioritization score: demand evidence 30%, value/quality evidence 25%, trend momentum 20%, affiliate potential 15%, content potential 10%.

This is a working hypothesis, not an objective product-quality rating. Store evidence, source, date and confidence behind each component.
